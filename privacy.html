Privacy policy

rclone cloud perso is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • It collects no data. It has no server, no analytics and no third party.
  • It reads and writes, in the owner's own Google Drive, only the files it created itself.
  • Data obtained from Google APIs stays on the owner's computer and in his Google Drive. It is never shared, sold or transferred.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
